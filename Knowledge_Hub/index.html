What Is Technology?

Technology involves the use of scientific knowledge to develop tools, machines, and systems that solve problems.

Computers, smartphones, the Internet, and artificial intelligence are examples of modern technology.

A Glimpse of Programming

Programmers write instructions that computers can execute. Here is a simple Java statement: 

System.out.println("Hello, World!");

Expected Output: Hello, World!

Useful Keyboard Shortcuts

Paste copied text: Ctrl + V

Understanding Variables

04 | MYTHS VS FACTS

Separate common misconceptions from reliable facts.

Myth Number 1

Humans use only 10% of their brains.

Fact: People use many different brain regions for different functions throughout everyday life.

Myth Number 2

All bacteria cause diseases.

Fact: Many bacteria are harmless, and some play important roles in digestion and ecosystems.

Technology Update

Pluto is classified as a dwarf planet.
